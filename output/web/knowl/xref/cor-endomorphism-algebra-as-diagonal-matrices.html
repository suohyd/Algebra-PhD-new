Corollary 4.6.4.

Let \(M_1,M_2,\cdots,M_n\) be right \(A\)-modules such that for \(i\neq j\text{,}\) \(\Hom{A}{M_j}{M_i}=0\text{.}\) Then,
\begin{align*} \End{A}{M_1\oplus M_2\oplus\cdots\oplus M_n}\amp\simeq\End{A}{M_1}\oplus\End{A}{M_2}\oplus\cdots\oplus\End{A}{M_n} \\ \amp \simeq\End{A}{M_1}\times\End{A}{M_2}\times\cdots\times\End{A}{M_n} \end{align*}
