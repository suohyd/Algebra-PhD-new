Theorem 4.8.1. Artin-Wedderburn.

Let \(A\) be a right or a left semisimple \(R\)-algebra.
  1. There exists natural numbers \(n_1,n_2,\ldots,n_r\) and \(R\)-division algebras \(D_1,D_2,\ldots,D_r\) such that
    \begin{equation} A\simeq M_{n_1}(D_1)\times M_{n_2}(D_2)\times\cdots\times M_{n_r}(D_r).\tag{4.8.1} \end{equation}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The pairs \((n_1,D_1),(n_2,D_2),\ldots,(n_r,D_r)\) in (4.8.1) are unique, up to an isomorphism.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Conversely, if \(n_1,n_2,\ldots,n_r\in\N\) and \(D_1,D_2,\ldots,D_r\) are division algebras over \(R\) then,
    \begin{equation*} M_{n_1}(D_1)\times M_{n_2}(D_2)\times\cdots\times M_{n_r}(D_r) \end{equation*}
    is a right and a left semisimple \(R\)-algebra.
    🔗
    🔗
