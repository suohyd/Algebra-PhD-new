Proposition 4.3.5.

Let \(A\) be a semisimple algbera and \(M,N\) be minimal right ideals of \(A\text{.}\) The following are equivalent:
  1. As \(A\)-modules, \(M\simeq N\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(MN\neq 0\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. there is an element \(a\in A\) such that \(aN=M\text{.}\)
