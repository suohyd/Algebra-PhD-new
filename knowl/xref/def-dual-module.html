Definition 3.8.16. Dual module.

Let \(A\) be a finite-dimensional algebra over a field \(F\text{.}\) Let \(M\) be a right \(A\)-module. We denote by \(M^*\) the dual space of \(M\) over \(F\text{,}\) i.e., \(M^*=\Hom{F}{M}{F}\text{.}\) We make \(M^*\) a left \(A\)-module by defining for \(a\in A\text{,}\) \(f\in M^*\text{,}\) and \(m\in M\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} (a\cdot f)(m) = f(m\cdot a). \end{equation*}
