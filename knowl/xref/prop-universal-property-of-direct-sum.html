Proposition 3.4.5. Direct sum - Existence and universal property.

Let \(S=\{M_j\}_{j\in J}\) be a nonempty collection of right \(A\)-modules. There exists a right \(A\)-module \(M\) with \(A\)-module homomorphisms \(i_j\colon M_j\to M\) for every \(j\in J\text{,}\) and with the following universal property: if there exists a right \(A\)-module \(N\) with \(f_j\colon M_j\to N\) for every \(j\in J\) then there exists a unique \(A\)-module homomorphism \(f\colon M\to N\) such that \(f\circ i_j=f_j\) for each \(j\in J\text{.}\)
