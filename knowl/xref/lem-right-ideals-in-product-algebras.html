Lemma 4.3.1.

Let \(A=A_1\times A_2\) be a product of \(R\)-algebras, and \(M\) be a right ideal of \(A_1\text{.}\) Then we have the following:
  1. \(M\) is a right ideal of \(A\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\End{A}{M}=\End{A_1}{M}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\Hom{A}{M}{A}=\Hom{A}{M}{A_1}=\Hom{A_1}{M}{A_1}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(S\left(M_{A_1}\right)=S\left(M_A\right)\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(M\) is a minimal right ideal of \(A_1\) if and only if it is a minimal right ideal of \(A\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. Every minimal right ideal of \(A\) is a minimal right ideal of either \(A_1\) or \(A_2\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
