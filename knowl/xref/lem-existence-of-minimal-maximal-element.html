Lemma 3.6.3.

Following are equivalent for an \(A\)-module \(M\text{.}\)
  1. The module \(M\) is Noetherian (resp. Artinian);
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Every nonempty subset of \(S(M)\) has a maximal (resp., minimal) element.
    🔗
    🔗
Furthermore, \(M\) is Noetherian if and only if every \(A\)-submodule of \(M\) is finitely generated.
